# Vrinda-Store-Sales-Analysis
Excel dashboard analyzing Vrinda Store's 2022 annual sales across channels, states, categories, age groups, and gender.



Vrinda Store Sales Analysis
An annual sales report for Vrinda Store for the year 2022. The goal was to understand who their customers are, which channels drive the most orders, which states perform best, and how sales trend across the year — all built inside Excel using pivot tables and charts.

Dataset
31,000+ rows of order-level data
Covers the full year 2022 (Jan — Dec)
Columns: Order ID, Customer ID, Gender, Age, Age Group, Date, Month, Status, Channel, Category, Size, Quantity, Amount, Ship State, B2B
What I did
Cleaned and structured raw store data in Excel
Built pivot tables for each analysis
Created an interactive Excel dashboard with slicers
Key Findings
Women bought significantly more than men — ₹1.35Cr vs ₹76L
Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh were the top 3 states by sales
Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart together drove the majority of orders
Adult age group placed the highest number of orders compared to teenagers and seniors
March had the highest sales and orders across the year
Around 92% of orders were successfully delivered
Tools Used
Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers, Dashboard
